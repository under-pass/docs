<Update label="October 2026" description="v1.3">
  **Waiting room.** `POST /api/checkout/queue`, `GET|DELETE /api/checkout/queue/{id}`;
  `event.waiting_room`, `waiting_room_opens_at`, `waiting_room_rate`, `waiting_room_minutes`;
  `queue_pass` on holds and `checkout/start`. Events with the room on answer `403 QUEUE_PASS_REQUIRED`
  to a reservation without a turn. [Guide](/guides/waiting-room).

  *Oct 20:* the line is now a continuous serving number. The entry carries `number` (the queue
  number) and `poll_after_ms` (when to poll again); polling is a plain read, admission no longer
  depends on it, and nobody is dropped for not polling. Storefronts that poll on their own clock
  keep working.

  **Numbered seating.** `locality.seating`; `GET /api/events/public/{slug}/seating`,
  `…/seating/occupancy`, `POST …/seating/best-available`; `seat_ids` on hold and checkout items;
  `seat_label` on tickets; `409 SEAT_TAKEN`. Items without seats get the best available.
  [Guide](/guides/seating).

  **Multi-date events.** `performances[]` on the event payload, `?performance_id=` on the slug
  route, `performance_id` on items (required only when there are several dates,
  `400 PERFORMANCE_REQUIRED`). [Guide](/guides/performances).

  **Reservations.** `POST /api/checkout/holds`, `GET|PATCH|DELETE /api/checkout/holds/{id}`,
  `hold_id` on `checkout/start`, `event.hold_minutes`; `available` is now net of other buyers'
  holds and `reserved` is reported. `409 HOLD_EXPIRED` / `HOLD_NOT_ACTIVE`. [Guide](/guides/reservations).

  **Order status** gained `PARTIALLY_PAID`, `remaining_amount`, `total_installments`,
  `paid_installments`, `payments[]`, ticket objects, `event` and `customer`.
</Update>

<Update label="September – October 2026" description="v1.2">
  **Bar perks.** `ticketType.barPerks[]` on the event payload; `bar_perks[]` on `checkout/start`
  for purchasable perks; `BAR_PERK_*` error codes. [Guide](/guides/bar-perks).

  **Full-funnel pixels.** `ViewContent`, `AddToCart`, `InitiateCheckout` (on `checkout/start`
  return, keyed by `order_id`) and `Purchase` / `CompletePayment` (first payment), with
  `contents`; server-side counterparts on Meta CAPI and TikTok Events API deduplicated by the
  order id; advanced matching. [Contract](/analytics-pixels).

  **Marketing attribution.** `tracking` accepts `utm_*`, `gclid`, `fbclid`, `ttclid`, `ttp`,
  `referrer`, `landing_page`, `channel`. [Guide](/guides/attribution).

  **Ticket attributes.** `entry_cutoff`, `late_fee`, `min_consumption` on ticket types.
  [Guide](/guides/ticket-attributes).
</Update>

<Update label="September 2026" description="v1.1">
  **Attendees.** `items[].attendees` issues each ticket to a person; `event.require_individual_data`;
  `400 ATTENDEE_COUNT_MISMATCH`. [Guide](/guides/group-tickets-and-attendees).

  **ePayco** documented as the second payment provider: `payment_provider` is `bold`, `epayco`
  or `FREE`. Purchase limits (`max_tickets_per_order`, `max_tickets_per_email`,
  `ticket_type.max_per_order`) have been in v1.0 since August.
</Update>
