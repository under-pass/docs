---
title: "Waiting room"
description: "High-demand on-sales: a fair line in front of the tickets, with a pass you must present to reserve"
---

An organizer can turn on a **waiting room** for an event (`event.waiting_room === true`). From
then on nobody reserves without an admitted pass: `POST /api/checkout/holds` answers
`403 QUEUE_PASS_REQUIRED`, and so does `checkout/start` without a `hold_id`. Your storefront
must put the buyer in the line, poll their place, and present the pass once admitted.

Events without the room (the default) are unaffected. A storefront that never implements this
page keeps selling every event that has no room.

## How the line works

- **Before `waiting_room_opens_at`** everyone pre-queues with no position. When the doors open
  the pre-queue is **drawn at random**: arriving at 7:00 or 7:59 gives the same chance.
- **After opening**, first come first served: every joiner takes the next number. Admission
  runs at `waiting_room_rate` people per minute, continuously — think of a serving number that
  advances `rate / 60` every second; you are in the moment it reaches your number. An admitted
  pass lives `waiting_room_minutes` from that moment.
- `waiting_room_opens_at` may be `null`: the room is open from the start and only paces the
  flow.

All three values are on the public event payload, so you can show "abre en 12:34" and an
estimate.

## Endpoints

Same **session key** as [holds](/guides/reservations): the pass belongs to the browser that
queued, and only that browser's holds may use it.

| Verb | Path | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POST` | `/api/checkout/queue` | Join, or come back to, the line (`{ event_id, session_key }`) |
| `GET` | `/api/checkout/queue/{id}?session_key=…` | Poll the place. Cheap on both sides: the answer is arithmetic, and `poll_after_ms` tells you when to ask again. |
| `DELETE` | `/api/checkout/queue/{id}?session_key=…` | Leave |

### The entry

```json
{
  "id": "qe_…",               // the pass once status is ADMITTED
  "event_id": "…",
  "status": "WAITING",        // WAITING | ADMITTED | EXPIRED | LEFT
  "number": 1237,             // the queue number; null while pre-queuing (not drawn yet)
  "position": 37,             // 1-based; null while pre-queuing or once admitted
  "waiting_ahead": 36,
  "estimated_minutes": 2,
  "open": true,               // doors open?
  "opens_at": null,
  "admitted_at": null,
  "pass_expires_at": null,
  "rate": 60,
  "poll_after_ms": 8200       // when to poll again (3 s near the front, up to 60 s far back)
}
```

A quiet room admits on the spot: the first response may already be `ADMITTED`.

## Implementation

1. On the tickets page of an event with `waiting_room`, call `POST /api/checkout/queue` with
   the session key. Keep `id` in `sessionStorage` so a reload keeps the place.
2. While `WAITING`, hide the tickets and poll `GET /api/checkout/queue/{id}` after
   `poll_after_ms` (fall back to 5 seconds if you ignore it). Show `position`,
   `estimated_minutes`, and a countdown to `opens_at` when `open` is false. Nobody is dropped
   for not polling: your number keeps its place, and if it has already been served when you
   come back, the entry simply reads `ADMITTED` or `EXPIRED`.
3. When `status` becomes `ADMITTED`, show the tickets and a clock to `pass_expires_at`.
4. Send `queue_pass: entry.id` on `POST /api/checkout/holds`. The pass stays valid until it
   expires, so the buyer may change their selection.
5. `checkout/start` with `hold_id` needs no pass: the hold already went through the line. If
   you reserve inline (no hold), send `queue_pass` on `checkout/start` instead.

### When the pass is refused

`403 QUEUE_PASS_INVALID` means the pass expired, belongs to another session, or is for
another event. Call `POST /api/checkout/queue` again: an expired or left entry re-joins at
the back of the line. Never leave the buyer on a dead button.

`400 QUEUE_NOT_ENABLED` means you joined a room the event does not have; just show the
tickets.
